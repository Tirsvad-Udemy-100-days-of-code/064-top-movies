# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-10)

First release: the five requirements of Day 64 "Top Movies" of Udemy 100 Days of Code, with tests, a README and a wiki in English, Danish and Bahasa Malaysia.

### Added

- Home page that lists the movies stored in SQLite (requirement 1).
- Edit form for rating and review (requirement 2).
- Delete button on each movie card (requirement 3).
- Add Movie flow that searches The Movie Database, stores the chosen movie and opens its edit page (requirement 4).
- Ranking of the movies by rating on the home page (requirement 5).
- pytest suite (unit, integration and end-to-end), a local opengrep scan and a Docker screenshot tool.
- Wiki pages and screenshots for every lecture step in three languages.
